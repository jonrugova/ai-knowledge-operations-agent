SAMPLE / FICTIONAL DATA FOR PORTFOLIO DEMONSTRATION ONLY
Northstar Atelier

1. STANDARD PRODUCTION TIME
Standard orders are prepared in 12 calendar production days after payment and final details are confirmed. Production estimates are not guarantees.

2. SHIPPING WINDOW
After production, standard courier delivery usually takes 4–7 business days. The courier may experience delays outside Northstar Atelier's control.

3. STANDARD-SIZE RETURNS
Eligible standard-size items may be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery if unworn, undamaged, and accompanied by the original packaging. Return approval is required before sending an item back.

4. CUSTOM-SIZE RETURNS
Items made to a customer's measurements are final sale and are not eligible for routine returns or exchanges. Contact support if an item arrives damaged or materially differs from the confirmed measurements.

5. CUSTOMIZATION APPROVAL
Customization requests must be confirmed in writing before production starts. A confirmed customization may affect the production estimate and price.

6. ADDRESS CHANGES
An address change request should be submitted as soon as possible. Changes cannot be guaranteed after an order enters fulfillment, and any additional courier charge may be the customer's responsibility.

7. RUSH REQUESTS
Northstar Atelier does not promise rush production. Employees may submit a rush request for human review, but the request is not approved until an authorized approver records a decision.

8. DUTIES AND TAXES
Import duties, local taxes, and brokerage charges are determined by the destination country and are the customer's responsibility unless a written order confirmation states otherwise.

9. CANCELLATION
Cancellation requests are reviewed based on production status. Once production has started, cancellation may not be available.

10. GIFT WRAPPING
Northstar Atelier does not currently offer gift-wrapping service. Employees should not promise gift wrapping or add it to an order.
